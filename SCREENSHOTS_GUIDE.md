## 📷 Screenshots to Capture for Submission

Cognevance requires screenshots/demo as part of the submission package. Here is the recommended list of screenshots to capture:

### 1. 🏠 Home Page & Course Catalog (`screenshot_1_home.png`)
- **How to view:** Open `http://localhost:3005` in browser.
- **What it showcases:** 
  - EduFlow Header & Brand Logo
  - Hero banner with stats (6+ Courses, 5k+ Students, 4.9★ Rating)
  - Search bar and category filter tabs (Web Development, AI, Cloud, UI/UX, etc.)
  - Grid of rich course cards with ratings, instructor avatars, and duration tags.

### 2. 🔐 Authentication & Quick Demo Login (`screenshot_2_auth.png`)
- **How to view:** Click **"Sign In"** or **"Sign Up Free"** in the navbar.
- **What it showcases:**
  - Modern glassmorphic modal with tab switcher between Login and Register.
  - Role picker (Student Learner vs. Admin Instructor).
  - Quick demo 1-click pre-fill buttons for instant testing.

### 3. 🎓 Student Learning Hub (`screenshot_3_student_dashboard.png`)
- **How to view:** Click **"Demo: Student"** in the top navbar.
- **What it showcases:**
  - Personalized welcome greeting for *Rahul Verma (Student)*.
  - Learning statistics metrics (Enrolled Courses, In Progress, Completed, Avg. Completion %).
  - Enrolled course cards with dynamic progress bars and "Continue Learning" button.

### 4. 🎬 Interactive Course Player & Live Progress Tracker (`screenshot_4_course_player.png`)
- **How to view:** Click **"Continue Learning"** on any enrolled course.
- **What it showcases:**
  - HD Video player interface with lesson title and duration.
  - Syllabus playlist on the right with interactive checkboxes.
  - Live progress recalculation (e.g. from 25% -> 50% -> 75% -> 100%).
  - Completion celebration badge when 100% finished.

### 5. ⚡ Admin Studio & Financial Analytics (`screenshot_5_admin_studio.png`)
- **How to view:** Click **"Demo: Admin"** in the top navbar.
- **What it showcases:**
  - Metrics cards: Registered Students, Published Courses, Total Enrollments, Platform Revenue ($).
  - Course Management table with actions (Edit, Delete).
  - Recent Student Enrollments live feed.

### 6. ➕ Create / Edit Course Modal (`screenshot_6_admin_create_course.png`)
- **How to view:** In Admin Studio, click **"Publish New Course"**.
- **What it showcases:**
  - Course title, category, difficulty level, price, thumbnail, description.
  - Dynamic module builder (Add/remove lessons).
  - Clean form validation.
